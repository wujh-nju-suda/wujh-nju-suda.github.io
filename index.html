    Biography

    Jin-Hui Wu is a lecturer in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Soochow University and a member of Prof. Fanzhang Li's lab. His major research focuses on machine learning and its theories, including but not limited to the following.
    • Theories of neural networks, especially on complex-valued neural networks, flexible transmitter networks, and quantized neural networks.
    • Theories, algorithms, and applications of Lie group machine learning.
    Experiences:
    • Oct 2025 - Present: Lecturer, School of Computer Science and Technology, Soochow University.
    • Sep 2019 - Sep 2025: Ph.D., School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanjing University. (without entrance examination)
      Supervisors: Prof. Yuan Jiang and Prof. Zhi-Hua Zhou.
    • Sep 2015 - Jun 2019: B.Sc., Kuang Yaming Honors School, Nanjing University.
      Supervisor: Prof. Wei Wang.
